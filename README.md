# 👋 Hi, I'm Nihar

M.Sc. graduate in International Software System Science from Germany, building things that sit at the intersection of software engineering, cloud infrastructure, and machine learning. I like understanding systems from the ground up — from CPU architecture to Kubernetes clusters — and then building something real with that understanding.

Right now, I’m deep in self-directed projects that combine AI, cloud, and product thinking: an AI platform built from scratch with custom GPT, image and video diffusion models, and a Jarvis-style UI, alongside a full-stack career intelligence tool with its own cloud infrastructure. I’m also exploring PhD paths in generative AI for games and interactive systems.

- 🔭 Currently building **Sainyx** — a from-scratch AI platform (character-level GPT, image/video DDPM, Flask + HUD frontend)
- 🛠️ Also working on **CareerOps** — a career intelligence platform (Next.js + FastAPI, deployed on GCP with Terraform)
- 🌱 Learning by doing: I prefer building from first principles over wrapping existing APIs
- 🎮 Outside of code: Dragon Ball Z, anime, and action/fighting games (Tekken, Elden Ring, God of War, Mortal Kombat, Street Fighter) — they shape my design instincts and creative thinking
- 📍 Based in Bamberg, Germany
