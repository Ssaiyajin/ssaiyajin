# 👋 Hi, I'm Nihar

M.Sc. graduate in International Software System Science from Germany, building things at the intersection of software engineering, cloud infrastructure, and machine learning. I enjoy understanding systems from the ground up - from CPU architecture to Kubernetes clusters - and then turning that understanding into real products.

Right now, I’m building ambitious self-directed projects at the edge of AI and cloud: an AI platform from scratch with custom GPT, image and video diffusion models, and a Jarvis-style interface; alongside a full-stack career intelligence platform with its own cloud infrastructure. I’m also exploring PhD paths in generative AI for games and interactive systems.

- 🔭 Currently building **Sainyx** - a from-scratch AI platform (character-level GPT, image/video DDPM, Flask + HUD frontend)
- 🛠️ Also working on **CareerOps** - a career intelligence platform (Next.js + FastAPI, deployed on GCP with Terraform)
- 🌱 Learning by doing: I prefer building things from first principles over wrapping existing APIs
- 🎮 Outside of code: Dragon Ball Z, anime, and action/fighting games like Tekken, Elden Ring, God of War, Mortal Kombat, and Street Fighter - they shape my design instincts and creativity
- 📍 Based in Bamberg, Germany
- 💬 Open to Research Assistant / DevOps / Cloud / AI roles - feel free to reach out
